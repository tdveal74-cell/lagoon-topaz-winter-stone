# EditForge house

**Status:** Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.
The visitor asked to run scroll-craft on the entire EditForge, not another interview.

Evidence: EditForge is a finishing house. Canvas and Agent are working floors. Dailies, Timeline, and Rubric were documents. The landing is already split-stage Create vs Finish. Stills already exist. Navy, amber, paper, IBM Plex.

Assumptions marked as authored.

## Eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Finishing house. Quiet. Photographic. Restrained. References: a contact sheet on a bench, a bay at lunch, a still that can cut.
2. **Journey.** Threshold into a room. Canvas makes plates. Agent queues jobs. Dailies is the pile. Timeline is the cut. Rubric is the gate. The last thing is a held still, not a footer.
3. **Energy.** Canvas and Agent stay concentrated. Dailies is the peak. Timeline is pulse. Rubric is verdict. Home already holds the create/finish tension.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** The moment they remember: a still left the pile and sat on the cut. Peak lives in Dailies.
5. **One thing no site does.** The wheel is not a tour of a product shot. Each room is a different grammar, and the working floor stays a working floor. The stamp on Dailies is the only move they can describe to a friend.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal, already the house. Do not drift to cream-and-brass artisan.
7. **World.** Distinct rooms, not one unbroken flight. A worldflight would destroy the tools.
8. **Assets.** Existing EditForge stills in `/stills`. No new generation.

## Grammar

**House of rooms** (named). Nav is the department strip. Each room picks a kit grammar for its own page so the fingerprint does not collapse into the landing's split-stage.

| Room | Page grammar |
|---|---|
| Home | split stage (already shipped) |
| Canvas | live surface |
| Agent | live surface (Create sibling) |
| Dailies | gallery / catalog |
| Timeline | rhythmic cutlist |
| Rubric | typographic poster |

**Nav:** department strip. Wordmark plus rooms. No marketing CTA bar.
**Hero of the house:** the landing split, already shipped. Inside the house, object one of the roll, the live graph, GATE at scale.
**Close of the house:** rubric gate bar. It lifts (paper) or holds (navy).
**Bans:** marketing journeys on working floors. Worldflight. Filmic one-shot as a house default. A second house peak. Invented statistics.

## Signature

**Stamp.** A plate presses into a fixed strip. The strip is the cut, in miniature, and it stays.

## Fingerprint gate (against EditForge Canvas)

- Grammar: house of rooms vs split stage

- Hero: per-room (graph, object one, GATE) vs 50/50 rooms
- Act-sequence: rooms with distinct scores vs pin, flow, pin, flow, pin · 5 acts, 8.8vh
