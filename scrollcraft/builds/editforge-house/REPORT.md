## Grammar
**House of rooms.** Each department keeps its own kit grammar. Working floors stay working floors.

Other seven lost as a house default:
- Filmic one-shot: default trap, not a tool argument
- Split stage: taken by the landing
- Live surface as the whole site: that would erase Dailies, Timeline, Rubric
- Continuous world: not a geography, and it would destroy the tools
- Chaptered editorial: title pages fight a studio
- Gallery / poster / cutlist: each is used inside a room, not as the house
