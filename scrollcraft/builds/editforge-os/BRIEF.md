## Grammar decision

**Live surface.** The page is the bay. Scroll drives its state.

Why the other seven lost:
- Filmic one-shot: default trap, marketing carry, not a tool argument.
- Split stage: taken by EditForge Canvas. Create vs finish is that page's whole idea.
- Chaptered editorial: title-page-with-no-media fights a media studio.
- Continuous world: not a geography.
- Typographic poster: we have real plates and a real module list. Using them is the point.
- Gallery: options are not the question. The question is whether a take may leave.
- Rhythmic cutlist: house law is restraint. Pulse would lie.

**Nav:** app chrome. Status bar with bay label, department jumps, playhead. No wordmark-plus-CTA bar.
**Hero:** the surface already in a state. No claim laid over footage. No 6rem display.
**Close:** an actual input. Command field. Same label as every other ask: Enter studio. No magnet, no spotlight.

**Signature move:** The take stamp. INGEST, GRADE, RUBRIC marks land on a real still, driven from `--sc-p` in page CSS. Engine untouched.

**Fingerprint gate (against EditForge Canvas):**
- Grammar: live surface vs split stage
- Nav: status bar vs divider chrome
- Hero: idle bay vs 50/50 rooms
- Act-sequence: pin, flow, pin, pan, flow, pin · 6 acts, about 9.4vh vs pin, flow, pin, flow, pin · 5 acts, about 8.8vh
- Close: command field vs collapse to finish
- Signature: take stamp vs plate drop

Differs on 6 of 6.

## Score

| Beat | Device family | Span | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Recognition | `pin` | 1.6vh | Surface already running. Ground is the bay, so the empty-stage window is filled. |
| 2 Tension | `flow` + `in` | document | Job row. Hard cut of copy. Adjacent to pin so families do not repeat. |
| 3 The stamp (peak) | `pin` + custom `--sc-p` | 3.2vh | Largest span. Signature lives here. Quiet before it. |
| 4 Range | `pan` | 4.2vh | Department index as a labelled rail. Lateral travel reads as rooms, not argument. |
| 5 Law | `flow` + `count` | document | Real telemetry from `STUDIO_MODULES` and `videos()`. |
| 6 Close | `pin` | 1.4vh | Command field holds. Last cues are one-value. Footer inside the stage. |

Families used: pin, flow, pan, count. Four. No family twice in a row.
Scrub acts: zero (live surface bans scrub).
Kinetic, spotlight, magnet: banned here, unused.
Drift: none. Grounds painted per act. Live surface allows at most two drift stops; this page uses hard grounds instead.
Total ~10.4vh plus two flow sections. Outside the 6 to 7 act / 13.6vh band.

Aesthetic family: premium-minimal, earned by the house, not by taste default.
